About

Dr. Jeremy Velasco

PT, DPT, CPT, TPI

Owner / Physical Therapist

Jeremy Velasco is the founder of Elite Golf Physical Therapy and a San Diego native with a passion for helping golfers feel and play their best. Jeremy combines his expertise in rehabilitation and golf biomechanics to create customized programs that keep golfers pain-free and performing at their highest level.

Jeremy has also performed treatments on professional senior women LPGA golfers, bringing the same tour-level care he delivers to elite athletes into every program he writes for Elite Golf PT clients.

When he's not helping clients improve their game, Jeremy enjoys golfing, playing tennis, running, and exploring new destinations. He's also a foodie at heart and loves discovering unique culinary spots. At home, he's often spending time with his energetic Labrador, Mango.

Jeremy's vision for Elite Golf PT is simple: to provide the luxury of top-tier, professional-level care, typically reserved for elite golfers, and make it accessible to golf enthusiasts across San Diego. His goal is to help every client improve motion, build power, and golf better.

It depends on your condition and goals. After your initial evaluation, we'll recommend the package that fits your timeline best. We offer three structured plans:

  • Par Package

    6 sessions

    Great for golfers looking to ease pain, fine-tune key movements, and start a tailored golf-specific program. A good option for those looking to make small changes.

  • Birdie Package

    12 sessions

    Designed for golfers serious about improving mobility, strength, and swing mechanics. This program helps unlock more power, consistency, and performance on the course. A popular choice for players ready to elevate their game.

  • Eagle Package

    18 sessions

    For dedicated golfers committed to long-term success. This comprehensive program is ideal for addressing movement impairments, optimizing mechanics, and achieving peak performance. The best option for players looking for lasting results and their best golf yet.

Many patients see significant improvement within the first 4–8 sessions, regardless of which package they choose.
