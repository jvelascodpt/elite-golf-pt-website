What We See Most

Restrictions lead to
swing dysfunction.

For some golfers, it shows up as pain. For many more it starts quieter — swing compensations that don't get corrected lead to inadequate mechanics, resulting in pain and stiffness. Next thing you know, your body feels worse the days following.

Either way, you start swinging around it. That compensation is what keeps you in an endless loop.

Compensating through the swing

Your body routes around the restriction to get through the ball. The swing still happens, just on mechanics that cost you distance and accuracy.
